HBAR02 · fixed scale

  Client delivery  ███░░░░░    38%
  Admin            █░░░░░░░     9%

Bounded domains pin their scale. 9% cannot render as a full bar. Cells are the unit; the value is right-aligned.

MULTI05 · small multiples, one scale

Shared scale is the point. Three independent sparklines cannot be compared; three rows sharing a range can.

Encoding

ASCII FALLBACKsame ladder positions
  unicode  ▁▃▅▇█▇▅▃ temp 53→74°
  ascii    .-+#@#+- temp 53→74°

  unicode  █████░░░  62%
  ascii    #####...  62%

  marks    ▲ ● › ·        caret, on, lead-in, idle
           ^ o > .        ascii equivalents

.:-=+*#@ replaces the block ladder, -=#@ replaces the bar ladder, + - | = replaces box drawing. Degraded resolution, identical language, for SMS, logs and broken fonts.

RESERVED GLYPHSno second meaning
MarkAlready means
↑high temperature / increase
↓low temperature / decrease
▲caret: points at the column annotated below
›bullet / list lead-in
·separator or idle state
●active or present
[NORMAL]status pill
▁▂▃▄▅▆▇█data ladder: height means magnitude
▘▝▗▖motion only: never carries a value

A mark already spent on one meaning is not available for another. Wind arrows are the live collision.
